# Submission status — Season 1

Individual submissions and results are private. We do not publish candidate handles, repository
links, methods, scores, outcomes or review notes, including during the active season.

Candidates receive acknowledgement, status and feedback through their own private submission
thread. We may publish only aggregate queue size and median response time without identifying
candidates or exposing candidate-level metrics.

`leaderboard: false` is required in `submission.yaml`. Any later publication of an individual's
work or result requires a separate, explicit agreement after the season closes; submitting does
not provide that consent. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).
