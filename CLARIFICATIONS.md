# Season 1 clarifications

Submissions opened on 7 September 2026.
Use [CONTACT.md](CONTACT.md) for questions. Material task clarifications will be recorded here
with a date and the affected problem. A rule change will not be applied retroactively without
explaining its effect on existing submissions and allowing affected candidates to update their work.

## 7 September 2026 — private submissions (all problems)

Replaced public submission PRs with private intake and separate private solution
repositories. Individual results and methods are private; no candidate leaderboard is published.
SUBMISSION_SCHEMA.md revision 3 requires `repo_visibility: private` and `leaderboard: false`.
This changes delivery and privacy, not problem bars or required evidence.

## 7 September 2026 — candidate costs and third-party offers (all problems)

Deployment.inc does not provide candidate API/GPU credits, grants or reimbursements. Removed the
incorrect company-funded credit promise. US$50 is the submitted-run usage ceiling at pinned list
prices, not money provided to a candidate. FREE_CREDITS.md lists optional external provider offers
with official sources; those providers control eligibility and terms. Problem bars are unchanged.

## 7 September 2026 — submissions open and private form (all problems)

The [submission form](https://deployment.inc/hiring-challenges/#submit) is open. It collects contact
details, a short introduction, the chosen challenge, private repository URL, exact commit and YAML.
Cloudflare D1 stores entries privately and the form returns a delivery ID. CONTACT.md explains
reviewer access, updates and withdrawals; PRIVACY.md lists the data collected and retention.
SUBMISSION_SCHEMA.md revision 4 changes delivery instructions; required evidence and problem bars
are unchanged. The Season 1 targets and released file hashes are now published as the active release.
