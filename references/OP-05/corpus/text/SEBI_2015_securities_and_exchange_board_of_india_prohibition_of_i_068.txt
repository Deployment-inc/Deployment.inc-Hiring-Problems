[SEBI_2015_securities_and_exchange_board_of_india_prohibition_of_i_068#s19633]
The compliance officer shall approve or reject the trading plan within two trading days of the submission of the trading plan and communicate to the designated person. No trade shall be executed in terms of a trading plan until there is a cooling-off period of at least sixty days before the commencement of trades under such plan.

[SEBI_2015_securities_and_exchange_board_of_india_prohibition_of_i_068#s52707]
Every company shall notify the particulars of trades by designated persons to the stock exchange on which the securities are listed within two trading days of receipt of the disclosure or becoming aware of the trade, as the case may be.
