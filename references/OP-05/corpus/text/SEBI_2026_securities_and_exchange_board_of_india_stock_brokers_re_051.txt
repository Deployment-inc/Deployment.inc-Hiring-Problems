The trading member/clearing member/self-clearing member/professional clearing member shall comply with the following minimum net worth requirements: Trading Member — 1 crore; Self-Clearing Member — 5 crore; Clearing Member — 15 crore; Professional Clearing Member — 50 crore. The stock broker may act as an underwriter only out of its own net worth/funds and the underwriting obligation shall not exceed twenty times of the net worth.
