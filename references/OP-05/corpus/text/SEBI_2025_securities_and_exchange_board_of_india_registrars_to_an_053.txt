Every registrar to an issue and share transfer agent shall maintain the records relating to each issue for a minimum period of eight years after completion of the issue. In the event of cancellation or surrender of the certificate of registration, the registrar shall ensure that all records are transferred to the respective issuer company or another registered share transfer agent within thirty days.
